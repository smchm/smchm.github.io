Post-programming desperation initially led to DevOps/SRE shenanigans with K8s, pipelines and limited fun. Chips(UK edition) fill the gap, and here's the sauce:

### Ingredients

- ½ tin of baked beans
- Handful of golden raisins
- Some curry powder

### Method

1. Heat all the ingredients in a pan for about 5 minutes or until the raisins are soft. Stir constantly.
2. Blend with a hand blender adding hot water until the consistency is acceptable.
3. Serve (over chips)
